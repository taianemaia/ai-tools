**Title:** Product Search

**Story:**
As a shopper, I want to search for products by keyword, so that I can quickly find items I am interested in purchasing.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given I am on any page of the store, when I enter a keyword and submit, then I am taken to a results page showing matching products.
2. Each result displays: product name, thumbnail, price, and a link to the product detail page.
3. If no results are found, a clear message is shown with suggestions (check spelling, try broader terms, browse categories).
4. Results load within 2 seconds under normal conditions.
5. I can sort results by relevance, price (low-high / high-low), and newest arrivals.
6. The total number of matching products is visible.
7. Autocomplete suggestions (up to 5) appear after 3+ characters are typed.
8. My search term is preserved in the search bar after submitting.
9. Search is case-insensitive.
10. On error, a user-friendly message is shown.

**INVEST:** Independent, Negotiable (autocomplete/sort can be descoped), Valuable (core to product discovery), Estimable, Small (no faceted filtering), Testable.

**Story Points:** 5 (medium — full-stack work: search bar, backend query, results UI, autocomplete, sorting, error handling).
